Payments

Simplify global contractor payments

Pay contractors accurately and on time across 200+ countries and jurisdictions and 120+ currencies. Deel centralizes invoicing, approvals, and global payouts, and handles payment escalations on your behalf, freeing you from manual follow-ups and issue management.

Automated invoicing

Digital invoices generate automatically based on contract terms and approved work. Fixed rate contracts invoice automatically, while PAYG and milestone work invoice after submission and approval, eliminating errors.

Pay globally in one transfer

Approve earnings and expenses, then pay contractors worldwide in a single bulk payment. Contractors receive funds using their chosen payout method, with near instant payout options supported, including Wise and Revolut.

15 flexible payout options

Let contractors withdraw earnings in local currencies using 15+ payout options, including Crypto, Wise, Revolut, and more. Contractors can choose what works best for them without extra setup on your side.

FAQs

Can I use Deel to onboard contractors internationally?

Yes. Deel lets you onboard and manage contractors in 200+ countries and jurisdictions with locally compliant contracts, built in classification safeguards, and global payments in one HR platform.

How does Deel help reduce contractor misclassification risk?

Deel embeds classification safeguards into contractor onboarding and engagement. By applying local labor law guidance and ongoing monitoring, Deel helps identify potential risks early and supports compliant contractor relationships.

How do contractor payments work with Deel?

Contractors submit invoices through Deel, which you can review and approve in one place. Payments are then sent globally in local currencies using 15+ payout options, including Crypto, Wise, and Revolut.

Can Deel support both contractors and employees?

Yes. Deel is designed to support contractors, employees, and employer of record workers in one system. You can manage contractors today and convert them to employees in 110+ countries as your business grows.

Is Deel suitable for small teams and large enterprises?

Yes. Deel supports fast growing startups and global enterprises alike, with workflows that scale across teams, countries, and compliance requirements.

Do contractors get their own experience in Deel?

Yes. Contractors get a dedicated portal where they can review contracts, submit invoices, track payments, and choose how they get paid.
