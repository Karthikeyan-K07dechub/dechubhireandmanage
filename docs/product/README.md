## Product statement

Dechub-Bridge helps companies hire, manage, pay, and support global workers through one guided workforce-operations service.

## Important rule

Do not present a service as automated, compliant, licensed, or globally available until Dechub-Bridge can actually deliver it directly or through a verified operating partner.
