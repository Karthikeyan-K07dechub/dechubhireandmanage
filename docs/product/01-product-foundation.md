# Product Foundation

## 1. Product definition

Dechub-Bridge is a workforce-operations platform and managed service for companies hiring or operating workers across borders. It is not primarily a project-management tool or a freelancer marketplace.

The platform connects the commercial journey and the operating journey:

`Lead -> qualified company -> service case -> worker engagement -> contract -> onboarding -> active workforce -> payroll and support -> offboarding`

Projects and tasks remain an optional delivery feature for project-based teams. They are not the main product message.

## 2. Customer problem

Companies can find talent, but cross-border engagement creates operational work:

- Sourcing and evaluating suitable workers.
- Selecting the right engagement model.
- Contracts, offer letters, worker documents, and approvals.
- Worker onboarding, equipment, system access, and support.
- Payroll, invoices, payments, and cost visibility.
- Worker classification, compliance, visa, and mobility coordination.
- Ongoing visibility across people, documents, and operational tasks.

The product reduces the handoffs between founders, HR, finance, recruiters, legal advisers, and workers.

## 3. Target market

| Area | Current position |
| --- | --- |
| Primary customer market | UAE companies and founders |
| Talent delivery | Global, with India as an important talent market |
| Expansion markets | India and the United States |
| Ideal company size | 1-50 and 50-200 employees |
| Initial sectors | SaaS, software, IT services, agencies, startups, healthcare organizations, and expansion-stage companies |
| Main buyers | Founder/CEO, HR/People leader, Head of Operations, Finance leader, hiring manager |

These sectors are initial focus areas because they commonly employ distributed technical or service teams, need speed, and have fewer internal workforce-operations resources. They are not permanent exclusions for other industries.

## 4. Services and delivery modes

### A. Managed workforce - recommended V1

Dechub-Bridge sources or introduces a worker, then manages the engagement process.

- Talent sourcing and shortlisted candidates.
- Contractor or employee engagement setup.
- Contract and worker document coordination.
- Onboarding and operational support.
- Payroll or invoice coordination for Bridge-managed workers.
- Compliance, mobility, equipment, and IT support when included in the service case.

### B. Customer workforce operations - later expansion

The client already has workers and asks Dechub-Bridge to run payroll, HR operations, compliance, equipment, or mobility.

This needs a distinct operating model because the platform must import existing worker data, prior payroll information, employer/entity information, statutory setup, and country-specific rules. It must not be assumed to work simply because the managed-workforce path exists.

## 5. Product promise

Primary message:

> Hire, manage, pay, and equip anyone, anywhere.

Operationally safe message while the product is growing:

> Access global talent and manage the workforce operations around every engagement through one guided Dechub-Bridge workspace.

## 6. Core objects and vocabulary

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Lead | A prospective customer from outreach, a demo form, referral, event, or partner. |
| Company | A customer organization with an account and company records. |
| Contact | A person at a lead or company. |
| Operations case | One request for a Dechub service: talent, payroll, EOR, compliance, visa, equipment, or IT access. |
| Talent request | A hiring-related operations case. This can remain a specialized view of an operations case. |
| Worker | A person engaged through Dechub-Bridge or managed for a customer. |
| Engagement | The legal and commercial relationship between a company and worker. |
| Contract | The agreement governing an engagement. A worker can have more than one contract over time. |
| Payroll run | A country/company/pay-period batch of approved worker payments. |
| Service task | An internal Dechub operational task, not a client delivery task. |
| Project task | Optional work-delivery task assigned to a project team. |

## 7. User roles

### V1

| User | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Prospect | Books a demo or communicates with Dechub. |
| Company Admin | One main client user who manages the company workspace. |
| Worker | Completes onboarding, documents, contract, payment details, and assigned work tasks. |
| Dechub Admin | Handles leads, client cases, workers, documents, support, and operations. |

### Later

Add company HR, finance, operations, viewer, recruiter, payroll specialist, compliance specialist, partner agency, and partner provider roles only when multiple active users require separate permissions.

## 8. Explicit non-goals for the first product release

- Becoming a general project-management tool.
- Building a full accounting system.
- Claiming automatic tax filing, payroll calculation, or legal compliance without a country-specific operating capability.
- Supporting every country from day one.
- Providing EOR or immigration services without the required legal entity, licence, or verified partner.
- Replacing a client HRIS immediately.
