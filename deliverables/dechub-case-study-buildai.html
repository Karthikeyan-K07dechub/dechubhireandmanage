How BuildAI streamlined global contractor onboarding with Dechub

A professional snapshot of how Dechub helps fast-moving teams replace fragmented contractor workflows with one operating layer for contracts, identity verification, invoice approval, and cross-border payouts.

Overview

The challenge

BuildAI, a Bengaluru-based product company hiring specialized US contractors, needed a faster way to issue agreements, collect signatures, verify worker identity, and release cross-border payments without creating new legal and finance bottlenecks every time a new contractor joined.

Before Dechub

Manual handoffs across contracts, onboarding, document collection, invoicing, and payouts slowed hiring momentum.

Primary risk

Each new contractor introduced compliance, payment coordination, and turnaround uncertainty for operations teams.

Business goal

Launch a repeatable hiring workflow that operations could run without depending on legal review for standard engagements.

Dechub solution footprint

  • Auto-generated contractor agreements for standard US engagements
  • Embedded DocuSign workflow for dual-party signatures
  • KYC identity verification with document and selfie checks
  • Invoice submission and approval in a shared dashboard
  • Wise-powered payout coordination across 170+ countries
  • Centralized document trail for contracts, KYC, invoices, and payment records
Execution

What changed in the workflow

1

Contract creation moved from ad hoc drafting to guided generation

BuildAI entered engagement details once and generated standardized contractor agreements without restarting the process with each hire.

2

Signature collection became a tracked, same-day digital flow

Contractors received the agreement, signed through DocuSign, and triggered a clear countersign process for the company.

3

KYC and payout setup became part of onboarding, not a separate project

Identity verification, bank details, and payment readiness were handled inside the same operating sequence instead of through disconnected tools.

4

Invoice approvals and payouts became operationally predictable

Once work was complete, contractors submitted invoices through their portal and BuildAI could approve payment from one dashboard.
